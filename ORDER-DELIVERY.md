# Agrogear order delivery details

The site does not send order emails or save delivery details. After the buyer
confirms payment and submits the delivery form, it prepares a message containing
the order, M-Pesa reference, and delivery address for that response. The buyer
must choose **Send order by WhatsApp** or **Send order by email**, then send the
pre-filled message from their own app to Agrogear.

The WhatsApp message is addressed to `+254781998896`; the email message is
addressed to `agrogear9@gmail.com`. If the buyer closes the page before sending,
the details are not delivered to the store.

Payments are still verified manually by the store.
